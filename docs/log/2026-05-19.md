## Decisions / changes from the plan

- **Manifest's per-page `space` field is compact.** The full paginated space raw lives in `spaces.json`; the per-page entry just records `{key, default_audience, checked_at, raw: {}}`. Avoids ~540KB of duplication across 34 pages.
- **`restriction_check` preserves insertion order** (`[direct, ancestors, space]`) — not alphabetized — matching the plan's example.
- **`restricted_to` (legacy puller-owned field) is NOT deleted by the probe.** Strict access-owned scope: only the three plan-listed fields are touched. Step 2 will drop `restricted_to` from the puller's schema in the same change that adopts the access-owned ownership.
- **Field-position fixup** — first frontmatter rewrite put `restriction_source_ids` at the tail (Python dict insertion order); a refined short-circuit catches the layout mismatch and reorders to `visibility_signal → restriction_check → restriction_source_ids` adjacency on re-run. Probe is now idempotent (third run: 0 changed).

## Resolved Phase 1.1 open questions (via Step 1 evidence)

1. **Space permissions visible to sync token?** Yes — 200 OK with full paginated raw.
2. **Puller dedupes ancestor lookups?** Titles yes, restrictions no (new helper added).
3. **User/group IDs returned to sync token?** **Full user objects** with `accountId`, `email`, `displayName`, etc. Manifest carries normalized `accountId`; raw preserved for re-parse. Future per-user expansion does not require admin-scope escalation.
4. **Errors to alerts?** Not yet — 0 errors on a 34-page corpus. Defer until corpus exceeds ~200 pages.

## Surprises

- **Direct restriction `raw` carries PII** (emails, display names) even when only an `accountId` was requested. Phase 1.1 mitigates by gitignoring the manifest and only ever surfacing folder-source names in the committed summary. Worth re-confirming this isolation when Step 2 ships.
- **Space permissions are heavily paginated** — 225 records across 9 pages of cursor pagination. The conservative heuristic ("non-empty results = `su_community`") is intentionally too coarse to detect a narrow space lockdown; deferred to a future ADR once we have evidence from a known-restricted SU space.
- **Page 544210961 ("Connect Claude to M365") flickered between `unknown` and `no_read_restrictions_seen`** across two runs of the probe. Looks like a transient API hiccup (network or 5xx) that the retry layer eventually swallowed. Worth keeping in mind for Step 2 — the puller's per-page sync should treat `unknown` as a non-fatal warning that prompts a re-classify on the next sync, not a hard failure.
